# CCA
**Executive Summary**

This project analyzes customer churn behavior using the telecom customer dataset to identify the major factors influencing customer retention and customer loss. The analysis was performed using Python libraries including Pandas, Matplotlib, and Seaborn.

The dataset was first cleaned and prepared by handling missing values in the TotalCharges column and transforming categorical values for better readability. Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) techniques and multiple visualizations were then used to uncover churn patterns and customer behavior trends.

**Key Findings**
Approximately 26.54% of customers have churned, indicating a significant customer loss rate.
Customer churn is almost equally distributed across genders, showing that gender does not strongly influence churn behavior.
Senior citizens have a comparatively higher churn rate than non-senior customers, suggesting this group may require better customer engagement and support strategies.
Customers with short tenure periods (1–2 months) are more likely to churn, while long-term customers tend to remain loyal.
Customers with month-to-month contracts show the highest churn rate, whereas customers with one-year or two-year contracts are more likely to stay with the company.
Service-related analysis revealed that customers lacking features such as:
Online Security
Tech Support
Device Protection
tend to churn more frequently.
Customers using Electronic Check as their payment method exhibit the highest churn probability compared to other payment methods.

**Conclusion**

The analysis highlights that customer churn is heavily influenced by contract type, tenure duration, support-related services, and payment methods. Customers with short-term commitments and limited service security/support are at greater risk of leaving the company.

**To improve customer retention, the company should:**

Encourage customers to shift toward long-term contracts
Improve onboarding for new customers
Promote value-added services such as Online Security and Tech Support
Investigate issues related to Electronic Check payment users
Design targeted retention strategies for senior citizens

Overall, this project demonstrates how data visualization and exploratory analysis can help businesses identify churn drivers and make data-driven decisions to improve customer retention.
